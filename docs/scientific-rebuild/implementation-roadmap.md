Every implementation stage requires separate authorization. Only the foundation branch exists now. Estimates are active engineer-weeks for one experienced developer, including validation, not elapsed deadlines. Access to sources and calibration data can add unbounded waiting; overlapping estimates are not a fixed-price promise. Preserve the recovery app as an independently runnable comparison throughout.

## Exactly one recommended next implementation experiment

**Five-state combined-slip RWD reference**, isolated from production. Candidate mathematical source: Dallas et al., *Control Barrier Functions for Shared Control and Vehicle Safety*, arXiv:2503.19994, nonlinear single-track/Fiala-brush formulation reported by Mission 12. Zhang et al. DOI 10.1080/00423114.2025.2522391 supplies broader motivation, not an equation specification currently verified here.

**Source readiness is a hard prerequisite.** Obtain a readable primary manuscript; freeze revision, equation numbers, units, parameter table and permitted operating domain. Verify whether its tire formulation determines both longitudinal and lateral forces from wheel slip. Mission 12's reported capacity `Fy,max=sqrt((mu Fz)^2-gamma Fx^2)` alone is not a complete tire law, and may prescribe Fx rather than resolve wheel slip. If the source cannot support rotor-state coupling as proposed, stop and revise the formulation selection before coding. Do not invent a missing brush equation and attribute it to Dallas. This uncertainty makes the experiment conditional, not an excuse to implement a different second model. The supplied DOCX is readable; inaccessible primary equations block future implementation readiness, not this architecture assessment.

Proposed state is [u,v,r,omega_f,omega_r], plus pose quadrature [X,Y,psi]. Front and rear rotors represent axle-total inertia, not one wheel carrying an axle load. Inputs are physical front steer, rear drive torque and front/rear brake demand with documented signed resisting torque. Use static loads m g b/L and m g a/L; flat road, constant mass/geometry, no suspension, no free steering column, no engine/clutch/gear/differential, no renderer/effects, yaw damper, output EMA, sleep or collisions. Configuration explicitly records m, Iz, a,b, radii, axle inertias, mu, tire stiffness and their provenance. No real vehicle preset inferred from artwork.

The body/rotor balances and contact-work sign are derived in [mechanical interface contract](mechanical-interface-contract.md). The isolated published tire law and its regularization/domain will complete those balances only after source review. A steady-state tire law is not empirically correct merely because it is published. Use one traceable parameter set across ordinary and high-slip tests, clearly synthetic if uncalibrated.

Use fixed-step classical RK4 provisionally, with two tire evaluations at each of four stages: nominally eight force evaluations/step, no recursive contact refinement. This is not an energy-preserving method, nor proof a stiff wheel mode is stable. Start within a documented nonzero-speed domain. Low-speed stopping/lock tests must either have a defined contact treatment or be explicitly outside this reference; the later simulator must solve them. Real-time evaluation cost is bounded; offline equilibrium/root searches may iterate under separate limits and do not belong in the time-step hot path.

Bound the next experiment to tire verification, one ordinary cornering family and one high-sideslip equilibrium/trajectory family, plus invariant/convergence/performance tests. Do not bundle a full recovery map, chaos study, engine or game integration into it. Those are research capabilities enabled by the architecture, not additional authorized next experiments.

## Test and decision specification for that experiment

1. Before vehicle integration, independently verify published pure/combined force samples, zero force at zero slip, units, signs, symmetry, unloading and saturation over the declared domain. Require contact power nonpositive for a memoryless passive law. Parameter names must match stiffness/load/friction meanings; do not invent a “peak” slider.
2. Evaluate all stage forces from an immutable state. Reverse tire evaluation order and mirror lateral variables/steer; straight symmetric motion must remain straight. Establish scaled floating-point tolerances from operation count, then disclose them before driving comparisons.
3. Free body/rotor motion must conserve intended momenta and kinetic energy within integration roundoff. With prescribed drive/brakes, account for input work, brake heat and tire-road work. Gearbox and tire storage energy do not exist in this first experiment. Quantify work residual convergence; never require RK4 to conserve energy exactly under finite forced steps.
4. Replay fixed tick-indexed inputs with identical state/configuration and deterministic iteration order. Record full state, contact forces, slip, normal loads, powers, counters and CPU timing. Rendering is absent; replay must not depend on wall time.
5. Compare 100/200/400/800 Hz and a finer independent offline solver. Assess normalized state error and invariant/work residuals at matched times; check time of events and qualitative stability, not only final heading. Estimate observed convergence on smooth intervals; mode boundaries need separate analysis. Choose error tolerances before tuning parameters, using an explicitly stated engineering accuracy target and refinement plateau. No blanket percentage pass threshold based on the legacy simulator.
6. Demonstrate ordinary cornering and at least one high-sideslip sustained motion or equilibrium emerging from the equations. A finite scripted slide alone is not a stable drift equilibrium. For an equilibrium, publish residual and local Jacobian stability. If none is found, distinguish source/parameter/search limitations from architectural failure before expanding scope.
7. Count tire calls, allocations, and operation maxima; measure mean/median/p95/p99/worst step duration on named CPU/Node/browser with warmup and matched runs. Initial engineering target p95 <1 ms at 200 Hz, not a literature guarantee. An algorithmically bounded cost still needs measured frame-budget feasibility later. Reject silent step dropping or lower fidelity to pass timing.

Accept only if source, invariants, combined-work, ordinary/high-slip convergence and cost gates all pass. Stop on energy creation, unexplained order/mirror bias, dependence on yaw damping, or sensitivity that disappears under refinement. Lack of convergence can require reconsidering integrator/tire stiffness; it does not authorize adaptive machinery comparable to the historical solver. No human driving or real-world fidelity claim is possible from the standalone experiment alone.

## How prescribed torque is replaced later

The rear axle torque port initially receives externally prescribed work, measured as integral T_r omega_r dt. In stage 2, split the rear axle into two independent physical inertias and contact patches. In stage 3 remove external authority at those same drive ports, add crank/input/carrier states and gear/clutch/differential constraints, and solve tire/chassis/shaft exchange consistently. Driver throttle supplies engine torque demand, not wheel torque. Rotor speed remains a dynamic state and is fed back to crank through the same reaction solution. Replace the external-work ledger with engine/starter work, losses and clutch/synchronizer heat. Do not retain the prescribed torque motor in parallel or attach an engine by merely multiplying its torque curve by a gear ratio.

## Research capability after validation

Future authorized studies may continue equilibria versus torque/steer, classify eigenvalues, define control-limited safe sets and search recoverability. A finite policy search certifies only its policy class/horizon, not global uncontrollability. Perturbation/Lyapunov studies require scaled norms, bounded/recurrent behavior and independent numerical replication. High-speed scenarios beyond the tire/flat-road calibration domain remain model hypotheses. An eventual negative result for chaos is scientifically valid.
