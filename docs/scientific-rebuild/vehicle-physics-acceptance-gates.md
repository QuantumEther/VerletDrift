# First isolated vehicle — numerical architecture and acceptance gates

**All vehicle thresholds below are PROPOSED ENGINEERING ACCEPTANCE THRESHOLDS, not executed results or validated universal physical limits.** Mission 22 executed only the safety tests in [patch validation](tmeasy-zero-load-patch-validation.md). Real DeLorean handling, stopping distances and drift fidelity require empirical calibration later.

## Numerical policy selected

One fixed-step scheduler owns elapsed time. Start h=.001 s, chosen as an engineering candidate from the source's native tire default, not a proven four-wheel stability rate. Native bodies/constraints use EULER_IMPLICIT_LINEARIZED; external tire forces are explicit. Use a bounded native linear/constraint solver with a declared 50-iteration maximum initially; record residuals, attempts and failures. The corner prototype must verify joint configuration and solver residual units. A failed solve is a failed step, not permission for unbounded contact iteration or state resets.

|Coherent ordering|Benefit|Cost/limitation|
|---|---|---|
|Native cached order: Synchronize tires → apply prior cache → Advance tires → body step|Matches inspected/tested framework orchestration|Fresh first step applies zero; force/current frame can have different ages; delayed singular output can be masked by deactivation; extra explicit lag|
|**Selected same-stage partitioned order:** snapshot → Synchronize all → Advance all once → collect/apply new wrench → body step|All tire forces use the same mechanical stage and pre-update bristles; no extra cached-wrench delay|Still explicit body/tire partitioning and old-bristle force; stiff contact and discontinuities require refinement tests; not a fully implicit coupled solve|

Tire internal h is set from master h **before every Advance** and asserted equal; do not rely on an unrelated default. Body/wheel state is integrated only by the native mechanical stepper. Tire bristle metres and body velocities/momenta are preserved across deliberate rate changes; no historical displacement is reinterpreted as velocity. Validate changes at an accepted tick boundary; never change h midway through one force stage.

No per-contact nonlinear equilibrium loop is added. Initialization-only static depth/preload solving is bounded and separate. Constraint/brake stick-slip impulses use declared capacities and shared inertias, with explicit rejection on nonconvergence. Prescribed C2 steering must provide angle/rate/acceleration consistently to the native moving constraint, and actuator work must be logged.

Rendering is absent initially. A later renderer reads accepted snapshots and interpolates position/quaternion; it cannot alter geometry/state or clock. A slow renderer must not drop simulation time or claim completed physics steps that never ran. Reproducibility records input functions, fixture hashes, h, accepted/rejected attempts and force/state time levels.

Initial admission envelope (proposed, to be frozen before implementation tests): flat rigid road, finite inputs, positive masses/inertias, valid source tire geometry/coefficient ranges; speeds 0–25 m/s and reverse down to -3 m/s; steer command up to .35 rad and .5 rad/s; heave within declared ±.10 m around ride position. These are test-domain bounds, not hard physical clipping. Stop/report an out-of-domain state. Negative computed tire support uses the safety policy, not a positive load floor.

## Test matrix

Mandatory means required before calling the first four-wheel research vehicle accepted. Some are prerequisites of earlier corner/straight-line milestones; no test is claimed to have passed now. Every trace must expose all mechanical states (or native constrained equivalents), all eight tire states, inputs, loads, wrenches and work ports.

`T_ref=mu_ref*Fz,rear,static*R0` is an experiment torque scale, not an extra traction cap or production engine curve. A speed-hold test driver is outside the physics kernel and must expose every applied command/work port. It cannot mutate velocities or add yaw stabilization.

Normalize position by L, linear speed by 10 m/s, angular speed by 10/R0 rad/s for wheel spin and 1 rad/s for chassis, angles by 1 rad, heave by .1 m, bristles by `Fn_nom*muscale/sigma0`; use a documented nonzero energy scale such as max(initial kinetic, accumulated absolute work, 1 J). Never normalize by a vanishing quantity to manufacture a pass. Test every state group, not just heading.

For long unstable drifting transients, matched short-horizon convergence and explicitly documented instability/event behavior are distinct from indefinite path equality. Do not label unexpected step sensitivity “chaos” without evidence. Full-contact power may be positive through storage/release or source approximations; log the complete signed ports. Fail unexplained mechanical ledger residual or unaccounted source, not merely a positive instantaneous sample. Global passivity remains unresolved.

## Required capabilities versus accepted approximations

Mandatory initially: safety-patched finite state, correct hub/rotor reaction, combined slip, native suspension/contact gravity balance, brake slip/lock, explicit reverse semantics, same-stage clock ownership, recontact tests and bounded measured four-wheel cost. The source's 1 m/s moment jump and reverse mode are visible conditional research approximations: they are not fixed now and must survive the practical gates without masking or arbitrary tuning.

Provisional acceptable approximations: empirical source tire curves, ideal vertical guides/zero caster, prescribed steering, no detailed temperature/wear/pressure dynamics or full local-spin law, and no formal tire passivity proof. Acceptance is limited to the declared research domain after the listed tests, not presumed from the single-tire patch. Parking torsion, measured tire calibration, realistic camber/caster/compliance, thermal/wear and steering-column/drivetrain dynamics remain future improvements. Production integration and real-world validation are separate later approvals.
