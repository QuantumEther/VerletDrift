\section{Scoring Dynamics \& Game Mechanics}

This section documents scoring calculations based on vehicle dynamics, providing gameplay feedback.

\subsection{Drift Score}

Drift score increases based on lateral acceleration and slip angle:

\begin{equation}
  \dot{\text{score}} = k_{\text{drift}} \cdot (|a_{\text{lat}}| + k_{\text{slip}} \cdot |\alpha|) \cdot v
  \label{eq:drift-score-rate}
\end{equation}

\textbf{Physical Meaning}: Higher lateral acceleration and larger slip angles contribute to drift score. Speed multiplier ensures high-speed drifts are rewarded more.

\textbf{Parameters}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $k_{\text{drift}}$ = drift score multiplier (tunable for difficulty)
  \item $k_{\text{slip}}$ = slip angle weight
  \item $v$ = current speed factor (normalized)
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Drift Multiplier}

Consecutive drifts without braking increase a multiplier:

\begin{equation}
  m(t) = 1 + k_m \cdot \min(t, t_{\text{max}})
  \label{eq:drift-multiplier}
\end{equation}

\textbf{Physical Meaning}: Maintaining drifts without correction increases the score multiplier, encouraging skillful, sustained drifting.

\textbf{Notes}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Multiplier caps at a maximum (typically 10x)
  \item Multiplier resets on brake or collision
  \item Provides dynamic difficulty scaling
\end{itemize}
