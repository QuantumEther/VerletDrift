\section{Drag \& Air Resistance}

This section documents drag force computation, which opposes motion and provides realistic resistance.

\subsection{Drag Force}

\begin{equation}
  F_{\text{drag}} = \frac{1}{2} \rho \cdot A_d \cdot C_d \cdot v^2
  \label{eq:drag-force}
\end{equation}

\textbf{Physical Meaning}: Drag force is proportional to the square of velocity, opposing the direction of motion. It models air resistance and rolling resistance combined.

\textbf{Parameters}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $\rho$ = air density ($\approx 1.225$ kg/m³ at sea level)
  \item $A_d$ = frontal area (m²)
  \item $C_d$ = drag coefficient (0.3–0.4 for vehicles)
  \item $v$ = vehicle speed (m/s)
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Notes}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Drag acts in the negative forward direction (opposes motion)
  \item Quadratic dependence makes drag significant at high speeds
  \item Simplified model; rolling resistance not separately computed
  \item Applied as acceleration: $a_{\text{drag}} = -F_{\text{drag}} / m$
\end{itemize}
