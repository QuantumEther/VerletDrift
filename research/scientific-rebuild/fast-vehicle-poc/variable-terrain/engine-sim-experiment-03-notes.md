# Natural procedural audio — Experiment 03

Serve the repository root with `python3 -m http.server 8000` and open `research/scientific-rebuild/fast-vehicle-poc/variable-terrain/engine-sim-hybrid-v3.html` through that server. Use Audio to select Procedural, Sample or Hybrid; Synthesis switches original/refined procedural voicing. The expandable component controls adjust exhaust, intake, mechanical tick and pops. Gains and voicing transitions are smoothed in the audio worklet.

The existing pipeline applies asymmetric exhaust saturation, adds high-frequency rasp and crackle, mixes pops and mechanical tick, and soft-clips the output. These are potential sources of coloration; their individual perceptual contribution has not been independently established. The refined setting reduces the exhaust shaper drive, mid-frequency emphasis, rasp, crackle and pop amplitude while retaining the firing-event and architecture-specific timing. It does not change the sample playback algorithm. There is no new random modulation or vehicle physics.

The layer controls adjust the final procedural mix; turbo, starter and driveline sounds remain separately audible. The original setting restores the prior worklet's voicing when all layer gains are at 100%. Both settings pass through Experiment 01's existing mix, reverb, compressor and safety output stage.

The changes are hypotheses for listening, not proof of greater realism. The single recording's unknown engine and limited RPM coverage remain; no multi-RPM recording, anti-aliasing redesign, phase locking, or independent component isolation study has been performed. In particular, the sample-only path retains the previous experiment's processing and should be judged against that reference. A level-matched listening comparison and spectral/aliasing measurements remain necessary before choosing defaults.

A Chromium browser smoke run executed 23 cases covering the three audio modes, acceleration/deceleration, stop/restart, five engine profiles and blend extremes. The measured output was finite and unclipped; the largest observed sample peak was 0.330. This smoke test does not independently measure distortion, aliasing, component balance or perceptual naturalness, and it does not establish that the new sound is an improvement. Reproduce it with `node research/scientific-rebuild/fast-vehicle-poc/variable-terrain/engine-sim-experiment-03-test.mjs`; its result file is written to `/tmp/experiment03-results.json`.
