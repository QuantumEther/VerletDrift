Physical and interface limitations
Body heave/pitch/roll and four vertical hubs are simulated; the reduced shared-horizontal mass model is not full 6-DOF dynamics. Wheel tilt shows the terrain contact frame, not a separate camber degree of freedom. Rigid coachwork uses Euler presentation of the small-angle body coordinates; spring endpoints use the exact model coordinates. No extra stabilization or appearance forces.
Rubber darkness reuses Experiment 10/11’s uncalibrated visualization, now driven by irreversible bristle loss rather than mechanical contact work (which can return stored elastic energy). No real tire calibration, pneumatic aligning moment or rolling-patch renewal is claimed. The fixed-ratio reference has no shifts; the experimental six-rotor mode adds synchronized forward/reverse requests. Selector-force mechanics, clutch cutaway and audio are omitted; no fabricated mechanism animation.
