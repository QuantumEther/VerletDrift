# Cumulative laboratory development

Each interactive successor must retain the validated capabilities of its mature predecessor unless a specific incompatibility is documented and an explicit migration is tested. Headless fixtures supplement the interactive laboratory; they must not replace its normal application.

For each successor, maintain a capability inventory covering automobile and laboratory modes, engine/clutch/gearbox/differential/brake controls, dynamic steering, continuous driving, 3D mechanical inspection and camera controls, dashboards, accepted-stage tire recording, world-anchored marks, reset/clear/visibility, and navigator routes. Verify inherited behavior with numerical and browser tests. Do not describe a successor as complete while applicable inherited capabilities are absent.

When replacing dynamics, use one authoritative state and one force/work ledger. Do not run predecessor and successor suspensions in parallel. Use a single coupled contact solve for tire, brake, drivetrain and steering reactions. Preserve predecessor experiments and reference tests. Document approximation boundaries and differences from the predecessor without inventing physical accuracy.
