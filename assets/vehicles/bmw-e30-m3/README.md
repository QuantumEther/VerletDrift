# BMW E30 M3 visual model

The second-generation BMW M3 road-car reference is modeled as a late-1980s E30 M3. Nominal published dimensions used by the visual specification are 4.345 m length, 1.680 m width, 2.562 m wheelbase, 1.415 m front track, and 1.430 m rear track. Artwork contours and details are an original stylized vector interpretation, not an OEM drawing, scan, or licensed BMW asset.

`body.svg` and `wheel.svg` are local static vector assets. Their transparent fender openings are centered on the visual wheel anchors, allowing the independently rendered wheels to steer. The simulator's physical geometry and tire contact points remain separate and unchanged by selecting this appearance.
