PROPOSAL 4 — RADIAL MENU Subsystem nodes radiate from a central hub. Click any node to open a modal sheet from the bottom with that subsystem's sliders. Global search above the hub highlights matching nodes and auto-opens the best match. Experimental — prioritizes discovery over speed. Best for: exploration & onboarding
▸ Pros / Cons

PROS

  • Visually distinctive, feels intentional
  • Subsystem selection is explicit and spatial
  • Modal focuses attention — no distraction
  • Global search highlights matching subsystems

CONS

  • Two-step access to any slider (click node, then slider)
  • Radial layout has high mouse travel distance
  • Modal hides the game viewport during tuning
  • Node labels truncate on small circles
