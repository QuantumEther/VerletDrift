PROPOSAL 3 — DUAL-PANEL Left panel shows your pinned favorites — the 8 sliders you actually touch during a session. Right panel is the full explorer: all sliders grouped by subsystem, collapsible, searchable. Pin (☆) any slider from the explorer and it flies into favorites. Optimized for live tuning while driving. Best for: rapid live tuning
▸ Pros / Cons

PROS

  • Instant access to frequently-touched sliders
  • Full explorer is just one click away
  • Favorites adapt to your workflow, not subsystems
  • Collapsible explorer hides rarely-used subsystems

CONS

  • Pin/unpin interaction adds cognitive overhead
  • Empty favorites state is confusing on first use
  • Two panels split attention during search
  • Max 8 favorites may feel arbitrary
